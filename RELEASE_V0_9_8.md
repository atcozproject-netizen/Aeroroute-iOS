# Aeroroute V0.9.8 – flight-game readability and progression
- A strict on-screen label budget (3–6 labels, based on zoom) keeps airport maps uncluttered at every scale.
- Prioritizes player-owned, currently focused and serviced-route airports over unrelated points.
- Ordinary airports use 4-character ICAO labels only; complete names remain in the detail panel. Only the focused airport can show a compact name on close zoom.
- All 2661 airports remain visible as selectable dots at their zoom-appropriate level.
- A permanent, compact next-level goal bar in the time HUD displays passenger progress toward the next aircraft tier.
- New animated and touch-transparent level-up celebration triggers only after earned passenger progression, not on saved game load.
- No per-frame airport label UI objects; map drawing and animations remain light for iPhone Safari.
